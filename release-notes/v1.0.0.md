First playable version of ClubDojo: a ninja village you host on your own local network.

## Downloads

| File | Run it on |
|---|---|
| **ClubDojo-Server-1.0.0.exe** | The host PC. A console window shows the address, the 6-character join code and, on first run, the Grandmaster (admin) login. |
| **ClubDojo-Player-1.0.0.exe** | Each laptop. Double-click to play; no install needed. |
| **ClubDojo-Admin-1.0.0.exe** | The host or any laptop. Log in with a Grandmaster account. |

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. No internet connection is needed once the files are downloaded.

## Getting started

1. Put **ClubDojo-Server-1.0.0.exe** in a folder you will keep, then run it. It saves the world in a `ClubDojo-Data` folder next to the exe. Back up by copying that folder.
2. When Windows Firewall asks, allow the server on **private networks**.
3. On each laptop, run **ClubDojo-Player-1.0.0.exe**. Pick the server under *Found on your network*, or type the address shown on the host, then enter the join code.
4. Create a ninja and play. The admin runs **ClubDojo-Admin-1.0.0.exe** and logs in with the login printed by the server. Change that password in **Players > Reset password**.

The files are not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Choose **More info > Run anyway**.

## What's in v1.0.0

**Players**
- Create a ninja (username, password, ninja name, gi color) or log in.
- Walk the Forest Village, Main Dojo, Ramen House, Sports Park and Grotto by clicking. Click a building to walk through its door, take the east gate to the Sports Park, or the south path down to the Grotto.
- Every room fits the window, whatever its size or shape, so exits on the edge of the map are always on screen. Exits on the edge have a sign with arrows, the path carries on past the edge, and the **Exits** buttons under the room name walk you to any exit in one click.
- Room chat with speech bubbles, global announcements, and emotes (keys 1–6).
- HUD with belt and XP progress, coins, login streak, room and online count, today's challenge, and mini map.
- Change your gi and headband colors. Your position and look are saved.

**Daily play and belts**
- **Daily reward:** the first visit each day pays coins and XP, more for every day in a row (day 7 pays 150 coins). Miss a day and the streak starts again. The 🔥 next to your coins is your streak.
- **Daily challenges:** three new challenges every day, the same for every ninja in the village: a play or win goal, a featured minigame, and something around the village (visit a place, chat, emote, bow to a friend). Each one pays coins and XP the moment it's done. See them in **Quests**.
- **Belt tests:** earn the XP, meet the test's goals (play a minigame, try different games, finish first, complete daily challenges, keep a streak), then click **Sensei Oru** in the Main Dojo to take the test. Passing pays coins and announces your new belt to the whole village with confetti. The quest card lights up when a test is ready.

**Look and feel**
- Outdoor rooms follow the host's clock: morning light, warm evenings, and a blue night where lanterns, windows and pavilions glow and fireflies come out. Leaves drift by during the day; dust floats in lamplight indoors and in the cave.
- Forest, rock or dark timber fills the space around a room, so nothing looks letterboxed.
- Walking through a door fades into the new room with its name and the time of day.
- Door signs always name the room they lead to (the Main Dojo and Ramen House doors said "Snow Plaza").

**Minigames**
- Click a pavilion in the Forest Village, the Sports Park or the Grotto to open a lobby, ready up with friends, and play. Every round is run by the server and pays out coins and XP.
- **Dojo Memory** (1–4 players): watch the scrolls glow, then repeat the sequence.
- **Fishing** (1 player): your ninja casts from the pier and the bobber follows your mouse (or finger). Hook up to three fish, bring them out of the water and click them off. Deeper fish are worth more, jellyfish shake your catch loose, Time Fish add seconds, catches in a row build a combo up to x3, a full hook pays a bonus, and the last 10 seconds are a feeding frenzy. Your best score and a catch journal of every species you've landed are saved on your computer.
- **Sled Race** (1–4 players): race your own ninja, scarf streaming behind, down a mountain run. Dodge rocks and rolling snowballs, grab coins, ride boost pads, slide across ice, and hit snow ramps to fly over trouble. Press Space in the air for a spin, or hold a direction for a flip; two tricks in one jump is a double. Hold Down to tuck for speed, tuck in behind a rival to draft, and skim past hazards for close-call bonuses.
  - Three courses a day (Pine Run in the morning, Frost Gully at sunset, Summit Dash at night by lantern light). Each is the same for everyone all day, so your best time and ghost race you on the exact same run.
  - Three daily challenges, bronze, silver and gold medal times, and seven sled styles to unlock (Cherry Racer, Bamboo, Glacier, Dragon, Shadow, Golden). Progress is kept on your computer; sled styles show on your own screen.
  - Sound effects, with a mute button.
- **Go-Kart Race** (1–4 players): three laps around the track in a behind-the-kart chase view, with every racer's name over their kart. Trees, tyre stacks and a start gantry line the track under a sky that turns with you. The screen shows your place, lap, time left and speed, and calls out each lap, the final lap and when you're going the wrong way. Hit a wall and you slide along it instead of stopping. First across the line wins.
  - CPU racers fill any empty places on the grid, so racing alone is still a race. They hold a steady line, take the boost pads and keep the race close.
  - Orange boost pads on the three straights give a burst of speed.
  - Drift: hold Space or Shift while turning. Blue sparks, then orange, charge a mini-turbo that fires when you let go.
  - Lap times: the screen shows your current lap, your best lap this race and your best ever, and calls out a new best lap and a new race record. Records are kept on your computer.
  - Scoring: 10 points for each ninja you beat, 5 for each CPU racer, 5 for finishing and 3 for the race's fastest lap.
- **Cart Surfer** (1–4 players, Grotto): ride the mine cart, grab coins and dodge rockfalls.
- **Tennis** (1–2 players, Sports Park): solo, call your shot and rally against a bot; with two players, play points against a friend.
- **Basketball** (1–4 players, Sports Park): time the shot meter; far shots are worth three.
- **Soccer** (1–4 players, Sports Park): 2v2 on a stadium pitch with your own ninja. Shoot where you run, dash to shove opponents off the ball, grab Speed Boots and Fire Shots, and settle draws with Golden Goal. Bots fill empty spots.
- **Football** (1–4 players, Sports Park): kick field goals with a sweeping aim meter.

**Grandmaster tools (admin client)**
- **Live World:** see who is in each room, teleport or kick ninjas, and send announcements.
- **Players:** search; give or remove coins and XP; set belt or role; mute, ban, and reset passwords.
- **Map Editor:** paint tiles with undo, save drafts, and publish. Ninjas in the room see the change right away. Place, move or remove minigame pavilions.
- **Minigames:** turn each game on or off and set its reward multiplier.
- **Logs:** chat, logins, and admin actions.
- **Server:** address, join code (with a button to make a new one), status, and one-click backup.

**Server**
- Runs on the local network: WebSocket on TCP 7777, auto-discovery on UDP 7778, up to 30 ninjas.
- Passwords are hashed and login is rate-limited. Chat and names are filtered.
- The server checks every move, chat message, and admin action.

**Automatic updates**
- The Server, Player and Admin apps check this release at launch and every hour and download a newer build themselves. Player and Admin ask before restarting (or install it when you close them). The Server waits until nobody is online.
- Player and Admin: press **Alt** for the menu bar, then **Updates > Check for updates** or **Reinstall latest version**. Server: start it with `--reinstall` or `--no-update`, or type `reinstall` in its console window while it runs.
- Files downloaded before the Sports Park update have no updater. Download them once more from this page; later builds update themselves.
- Player or Admin stuck on a window titled `find` after an update or reinstall? That build's updater could hang. Close the window, then download the Player and Admin files once more from this page. Builds from 8 November 2026 onward update themselves.
